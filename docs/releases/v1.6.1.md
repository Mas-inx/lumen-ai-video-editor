## Lumen 1.6.1: games recorded at the frame rate you asked for

### Fixed

- **A game recorded with its window in front of Lumen came out at about 30 frames a second, not 60,** and stuttered. Lumen captures through Chromium, which takes each frame on a timer. Windows 11 coarsens the timers of an app whose windows are all covered, which is Lumen's situation while you play over it, so a timer set for 60 a second fired about 32 times. On battery, Chromium doesn't ask for fine timers at all.
  - While it records, Lumen now keeps its timers fine, and hands them back to Windows when the recording stops.
  - A window is asked for twice the frame rate, so even coarse timers catch every frame. Chromium never sends a window faster than about 60, so this costs little.
  - Measured on a FiveM game drawing 110 frames a second, with Lumen covered: 32 frames a second before, 59 to 61 after, with 471 of 473 frames exactly 1/60 s apart. The game kept its 110, and Lumen used a few percent more processor time.
- **When the Copilot or an agent threw a recording away** (`stop_recording` with `discard`, or a studio take that failed), Lumen could stop with a "JavaScript error occurred in the main process" box. This only happened when the recording had one app's sound or click data.

### Still to know

A whole screen (rather than a window) recorded on battery may still come in at about 32 frames a second. [Limits →](https://github.com/Mas-inx/lumen-ai-video-editor/blob/main/docs/RECORDING.md#limits)

Lumen 1.1 and later update themselves: restart Lumen once this release is out.
