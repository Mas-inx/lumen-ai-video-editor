## Lumen 1.6.2: a volume for the preview that leaves the mix alone

### Added

- **Preview volume.** The speaker beside the master meter sets how loud Lumen plays while you edit.
  - Click it for the slider and mute. Scroll over it to nudge the level, or press **Shift M** to mute. It's in the command palette too.
  - Only what you hear changes: clip, track and master levels, the meter and every export stay as they are.
  - Sounds you audition in the media and sound libraries follow it.
  - It's remembered between sessions, for you rather than for the project. A muted preview shows an amber speaker, so a silent timeline is never a mystery.

### Changed

- **The preview plays at the export's level by default.** It used to play about 2 dB quieter, at a monitor level of 80% you couldn't see. At 100% it now sounds exactly like the export.

Lumen 1.1 and later update themselves: restart Lumen once this release is out.
