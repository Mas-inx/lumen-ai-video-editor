## Lumen 1.4: frames straight from other apps, exports that work on any graphics card, and transitions you can place

### Frame ingest

Another app on your computer can send Lumen finished frames, and Lumen encodes each shot once into a master clip in the media library. It was built for **GS Cinematic Studio** filming GTA V, and any renderer or capture tool can use it. [How it works →](https://github.com/Mas-inx/lumen-ai-video-editor/blob/main/docs/INGEST.md)

- **Switch it on** in *Integrations › Frame ingest*, which shows the address and token to copy. Agents use `ingest_start` and `ingest_status`.
- **Masters keep full colour** (VP9 4:4:4) in three qualities: *Master* (visually lossless), *Lossless* (every pixel exactly as sent) and *Compact*. They look the same on every graphics card.
- **Cues.** A sender can say what happens in a clip (lines said, cuts, footsteps), since a master has no sound. They show along the bottom of the clip, and one click turns them into markers.

### Exports that find a working encoder

- **Tried for real.** Before an export, the encoder gets a few frames at the export's size, because some drivers accept settings and then fail.
- **Fallbacks.** If the graphics card's encoder is missing or fails, the processor's takes over, even part-way through an export. The result says what happened.
- **4K at 60 fps** is asked for at the right codec levels.
- **Graphics and encoding** (Lumen menu) shows which graphics card Lumen runs on and every encoder, tried at 1080p 60 and 4K 60. On a computer with two cards you can pick the fast one.
- **Damaged video no longer stops an export.** The picture before the damaged part is held, and the export says where.

This was tested on Intel graphics. NVIDIA and AMD cards go through the same checks and fallbacks but were not tested on real hardware: if an export misbehaves, *Graphics and encoding › Copy report* is the thing to send.

### Transitions where you want them

A transition can sit over the end of the first clip, on the cut, or over the start of the second.

- **Drag** one to where two clips touch. The timeline shows where it will land: *Before*, *On the cut* or *After*.
- **Click** a transition on the timeline to move it, change its length or swap it.
- **Or select** the clips either side of a cut and click a transition in the panel.

### Tracking

*Track motion* on a piece of footage with nothing under it used to end in an error. Now you box what to follow and a title is pinned to it, ready to reword. If a title or picture already lies over the footage, that one follows instead.

### The Copilot

- **Two motion-graphic style skills**, ten skills built in now: fifteen named looks (flat vector, line art, isometric, Bauhaus, collage, liquid, synthwave, pixel art, HUD and more), with the rules that make each read as that style. The style rules are adapted from [mg-styles-15](https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/mg-styles-15).
- **Long tool calls no longer time out** for agents on Lumen's MCP server: a call still working after 20 seconds answers `running`, and `get_tool_result` collects it.
- **122 tools**, with `graphics_report`, `ingest_start` and `ingest_status` new.

### Fixed

- The full-screen button did nothing once you were in full screen. It now switches back, and menus and messages show while you're in it.
- *Text behind subject* with *any subject* failed with "Too many storage buffers in shader". It uses a model that runs on Windows graphics cards now.
- The spinner on progress messages sat in the corner of the message.
- Videos rendered in FiveM's browser (every MP4 GS Cinematic Studio wrote) now import and play.
- A clip could lose its last frame on the timeline.
- Version history could lose a version saved in the same millisecond as another.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/Mas-inx/lumen-ai-video-editor/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

Lumen 1.1 and later update themselves: restart Lumen once this release is out. Lumen 1.0.x needs this installer once.
