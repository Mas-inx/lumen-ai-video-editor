## Lumen 1.3: render previews, a Copilot that thinks out loud, and the effects you reach for every day

### Render previews

Heavy stretches now play smoothly, like in Premiere and Filmora.

- **The bar under the ruler** shows yellow where playback may not keep up and green where a stretch is rendered.
- **Ways to render:** press `Enter` to render in to out (or the whole timeline), render just the selected clips, or let Lumen render heavy stretches while you're idle.
- **Resolution:** render at Full, 1/2, 1/4 or 1/8. Lower resolutions are sharpened back up to the viewer on the GPU.
- **Staying valid:** renders stay until what's on screen changes, and undo brings them back.

The program monitor also has a **preview resolution** (Auto, Full, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8). Auto steps down while playing when the computer can't keep up.

### Smoother on modest computers

- The timeline and preview no longer redraw while nothing changes.
- Filmstrips and waveforms are painted once and reused.
- Zooming, scrolling and scrubbing do a fraction of the work they did.

### The Copilot

- **Chats per project:** start new ones, switch between them, rename or delete them, and pick up where you left off after a restart.
- **Its thinking** streams in live, then folds away under *Thought for 12s*.
- **Copy** any request, reply, thinking or code block.
- **The web:** it can search, read pages and see a page as a screenshot, and links in its replies show previews. Only public sites are reachable.
- **Skills:** know-how it loads before the work, in the same SKILL.md format as Claude. Eight are built in:
  - short-form editing;
  - motion design craft;
  - HyperFrames compositions;
  - text behind subject;
  - colour grading;
  - sound and music;
  - Blender 3D;
  - generation prompts.

  Add your own in *Integrations › Skills*, or switch on Claude Code's. Claude Code, Codex and any agent using Lumen's MCP server get them too.

### Effects you reach for every day

- **Text behind subject.** Right-click a clip › *Subject*. On-device AI cuts the person out and slips a title between them and the background, in one undo step. The same menu also:
  - cuts out the subject;
  - removes the background;
  - removes the person and keeps the background.
- **Motion presets** in the Animate tab, baked into keyframes you can edit:
  - camera moves: Ken Burns, push in, pull out, pans and tilts;
  - entrances and exits: slide, pop, fade, zoom, drop and spin;
  - emphasis at the playhead: punch, pulse, shake and wobble.
- **28 easing curves** per keyframe, or your own cubic-bezier.
- **Keyframes on the timeline:** drag them to retime, or click one to jump to it.
- **Find the beat** of a song: right-click › *Beat*. Its beats and bars show on the waveform and cuts snap to them. Lay the selected clips out on the beat, or add markers on the downbeats.
- **Caption styles** in *Text › Auto captions*: *Clean*, *Bold*, *Word by word* (key words in an accent colour) and *Boxed*.
- **Select many media files** to add, transcribe or remove them together.

### Better generations

The motion graphic templates were redesigned to look made, not generated. They use:

- considered type: Fraunces, Archivo, Syne, Space Grotesk and Instrument Serif;
- easing that differs per element;
- holds long enough to read.

Lower thirds, title cards and kinetic type come in several styles, and there are two new templates, *Pull quote* and *Chapter marker*.

### Also

- 11 new AI tools, for 119 in all.
- HyperFrames renders were slightly off-centre with Windows display scaling above 100%. They're now pixel-exact at any scaling.
- Captions skip silent copies of a clip and no longer caption the same speech twice.
- A render or generation that failed straight away no longer looks stuck on *running*.

Lumen 1.1 and later update themselves: restart Lumen once this release is out. Lumen 1.0.x needs this installer once.
