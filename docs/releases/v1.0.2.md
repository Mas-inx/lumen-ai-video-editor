## Lumen 1.0.2 — the AI can see and hear

Every AI brain in Lumen — an API model, your own Claude Code or Codex, or an outside agent over MCP — now shares one toolset of about 70 tools. It can **look at your video**, **listen to it**, inspect every clip and edit with all-or-nothing batches.

### Highlights

- **Eyes** — `get_frame` renders any moment exactly as the export will look; `get_contact_sheet` shows the whole edit as one labelled grid; `get_media_frames` looks inside footage before using it; `get_editor_screenshot` sees the editor as you do.
- **Ears** — `get_transcript` (what's said and when, after every cut), `analyze_audio` (loudness in LUFS, peaks, clipping and silences, per track, with plain advice) and `find_media` (searches inside transcripts).
- **Hands** — `batch_edit` applies several commands as one undo step that lands completely or not at all, plus `add_title`, `undo` / `redo`, `select_clips`, `playback`, `save_project` and `export_video` (you choose where the file goes).
- **You see what it saw** — the frames the AI looked at appear on that step in the Copilot chat.
- **Works with any vision model** — Claude gets pictures inside tool results, other models as a follow-up message; a model that can't take images is detected and carries on without them.

### Fixes

- The Copilot remembers earlier steps' tool results between turns.
- Markers can be edited and removed by the AI; removing something that doesn't exist now reports an error.
- One export at a time, from the dialog or the AI.
- Sound effects, music and voice land on their own tracks instead of the first free one.
- The Export dialog's preview and frame snapshots are frame-exact, 3D and motion-graphic renders included.

Full list: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Mas-inx/lumen-ai-video-editor/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
