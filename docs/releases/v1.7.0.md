## Lumen 1.7: light and lens effects, smart transitions, storyboards and ComfyUI

### Light and lens

A new *Light* section in Effects, drawn on the GPU after the grade and identical in the preview and the export.

- **Glow & Bloom** now blooms just the highlights, with a threshold, a radius and a colour. Glows made before keep their look.
- **Halation:** film's red-orange glow just outside bright edges.
- **God Rays:** shafts of light from a light you place, on the frame or off it, through the bright parts of the shot.
- **Lens Flare:** classic, anamorphic or starburst, with the lens's reflections across the frame. Flares and god rays can **follow the brightest light** in the shot, and fade when it goes out.
- **Light Leak** in five styles, and a **Vignette** with a size, a feather, a roundness, a centre you move and a colour.
- **Relight:** add a light, or move where the light comes from. Lumen measures the shot's depth on your computer (Depth Anything V2, downloaded once), so the light wraps around faces and objects.
- **Particles:** dust in the air (or only where the shot is lit, like a sunbeam), snow, rain, embers, bokeh, sparkles and confetti, in layers near and far that move at their own speeds.

### Colour

- **Correction before the grade:** a white balance that makes whites white (pick something that should be neutral, or press *Auto*), and levels.
- **Match to another shot:** lines a clip's shadows, midtones, highlights and colour cast up with a reference shot, and can copy its grade too.

### Smart transitions

A new section in Transitions ([#2](https://github.com/Mas-inx/lumen-ai-video-editor/issues/2)). Each reads the two shots at its cut — where their subjects are, how the camera moves, how the frames line up, how their colours differ — and carries one into the other.

- **Morph:** one shot melts into the next along how their content lines up.
- **Zoom Through:** into the outgoing shot's subject and out of the incoming one's.
- **Whip Pan:** a motion-blurred whip in the camera's own direction.
- **Luma Fade:** the next shot appears through its brightest parts first.
- **Colour Match:** the next shot starts in this one's colours and eases into its own.
- **Choose for every cut** picks the one that suits each cut, says why, and times it so the shots swap on the music's beat — without moving a cut.

### Particle titles and charts

Two new motion graphics in *Generate › Motion* ([#2](https://github.com/Mas-inx/lumen-ai-video-editor/issues/2)).

- **Particle title:** your title gathers out of thousands of particles sampled from its own letters, settles into crisp type and scatters at the end. Dust, sparks, embers or digital pixels.
- **Animated charts:** bar, line and donut, from your real numbers. Round scales, labels that fit, values that count up, one item picked out in the accent. Type the data as `Label: value` lines and see what was read as you type.

### Plan it, style it, brand it

- **Storyboard:** a new *Story* panel holds the plan — scenes in order, each with a script, a treatment, a length, its transition and what it still needs. The Copilot plans it with you, produces it scene by scene and revises one scene without rebuilding the rest.
- **Six house styles** that are more than colour: Editorial, Product commercial, Kinetic typography, Infographic, Cinematic and Documentary. Each sets type, motion, captions, transitions, a look and pacing.
- **Brand kits:** colours, typefaces, logos, title and caption styles, applied in one step.
- **B-roll from your own media:** the media already in the project, ranked for each sentence by what it's about.

### Clean up the edit

- **Repeated takes:** Lumen finds lines said more than once — false starts, repeats, stumbles — and suggests the take to keep, saying why. A take that says something the others don't is never the one suggested to go.
- **Check edit:** one click finds problems before you export, worst first, with one-click fixes where they're safe — gaps, offline media, captions that overlap or are too short to read, titles off the frame, black or frozen picture, clipping, loudness, sound cut off at a cut.
- **Speech cuts never cut a word,** and never through another speaker.
- **Reframing keeps each shot's subject in view** in vertical, square and portrait copies.

### ComfyUI

Connect your own ComfyUI in *Integrations › ComfyUI* ([#5](https://github.com/Mas-inx/lumen-ai-video-editor/issues/5)). Import workflows (API exports, normal saved workflows or PNGs), and run them from *Generate › ComfyUI*: Lumen finds each workflow's prompt, seed, steps, size and inputs, feeds them from the selected clip, the frame at the playhead or your media, and puts the results in Media. Lumen talks only to the address you set.

### Steadier on long, heavy projects

- **Memory stays within bounds** while you play, scrub and export ([#6](https://github.com/Mas-inx/lumen-ai-video-editor/issues/6)): long audio streams instead of being decoded whole, decoded pictures and sound stay within fixed budgets, and players and decoders far from the playhead are let go.
- **If the editor crashes, runs out of memory or stops responding,** Lumen offers to reload it — back onto your project, with your unsaved changes — instead of leaving a black window.
- Fixed: memory growing while long recordings played, exports keeping a decoder open for every clip they had passed, seams in denoised long recordings, and a cut-out subject that could vanish for a moment after an edit.
- **Copilot chats in the project Lumen starts with are kept now.** Since 1.5.0 they were never saved, and were gone once you opened another project.

### For the Copilot and agents

Twenty-four new tools, **157 tools** in all now: `auto_color`, `match_color`, `relight_clip`, `smart_transition`, `auto_transitions`, the storyboard and brand commands, `get_storyboard`, `list_styles`, `apply_style`, `suggest_broll`, `get_brand_kit`, `apply_brand_kit`, `find_repeated_takes`, `remove_speech`, `check_quality` and four ComfyUI tools. `render_motion_graphic` makes particle titles and charts. [Every tool →](https://github.com/Mas-inx/lumen-ai-video-editor/blob/main/docs/AI-TOOLS.md)

### What was and wasn't tested

This was built and checked on one Windows 11 laptop with Intel graphics.

- **The built app,** on a profile of its own: a chart and a particle title rendered through HyperFrames, a smart transition chosen and drawn at a cut, particles, and Relight shaped by the downloaded Depth Anything model. Starting it is also what turned up the Copilot chat bug above.
- **The editor in a browser:** every light effect, every smart transition at the start, middle and end of a cut, all seven kinds of particles, and *Generate › Motion*.
- **Particle titles and charts:** every style and kind came out the same frame for frame when rendered twice from scratch, and when a frame was jumped to directly.
- **741 unit tests,** among them reading a cut, beat timing, the correction maths, chart scales and number formats, the memory budgets, repeated takes, the edit check and ComfyUI's workflows.

Not tested for this release: NVIDIA and AMD graphics; ComfyUI against a real ComfyUI install (only a stand-in server); the memory limits on a real long, heavy project (they are tested against fakes); smart transitions on real footage with a moving camera (only still pictures); an export with the new effects; and Windows 10.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/Mas-inx/lumen-ai-video-editor/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
