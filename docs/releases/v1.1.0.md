## Lumen 1.1 — the pro update

Twenty-seven features that good editors have — pro trimming, a real mixer, GPU colour grading, proxies, multiple timelines, multicam, motion tracking, stabilization and more — and from this version on, **Lumen updates itself**.

### Editing

- **Roll, slip and slide** tools; ripple trim, insert and overwrite on any track.
- **In and out points**: play, lift or extract the marked stretch, or export just that part.
- **Copy, cut, paste** and *paste attributes*; **crop** with rounded corners; **freeze frames** and **speed ramps**.
- **Detached sound** for J- and L-cuts, **groups**, and solo and reorder for tracks.
- **Multiple timelines** — another cut, or a vertical copy of the whole edit reframed in one click — and **nesting**: turn any clips into one clip you can grade, speed up or cut, and step inside to edit.
- **Multicam**: pick the camera recordings, Lumen lines them up by their sound, and `1`–`9` cut between cameras as it plays, with every angle live under the preview.
- **Scene detection**, **stabilization** and **motion tracking** — titles, stickers and masks that follow something moving, including a blur that follows a face.

### Sound

- **A mixer**: a channel per track and a master, each with a fader, pan, live meters, an EQ you drag, a compressor and a limiter.
- **Crossfades** on transitions, and **loudness normalization** on export (−14, −16, −23 or −24 LUFS).
- Hour-long recordings **stream** instead of filling memory.

### Colour and compositing

- **GPU grading**: tone curves, lift / gamma / gain wheels, HSL by colour range, **.cube LUTs** — and **scopes** beside the monitor.
- **Chroma and luma keys**, feathered **masks** (on adjustment layers too), outlined titles in **any font**.

### Workflow

- **Proxies** for 4K footage, **version history** with automatic snapshots, and **Collect project**.
- **Subtitles**: import SRT and WebVTT files, export the captions as SRT or WebVTT, beside the video or on their own.
- **Render queue**: line exports up and keep editing while they render; **PNG frames** and **WebM with transparency** for compositing.

### AI

- **108 tools** for every AI brain (from 91) — everything above included.
- **OpenCode Go** as a Copilot brain, and a **model and effort picker** for every brain.

### Automatic updates

Lumen now checks this Releases page, downloads new versions in the background and installs them when you restart. **Lumen 1.0.x can't update itself, so install this version once by hand** — after that, updates arrive on their own.

### Fixes

- The taskbar, Start menu and desktop icons were blank after installing 1.0.2.
- Claude, GPT and Gemini models on OpenCode Zen now work.
- Sped-up video plays smoothly in the preview, the sound under a transition crossfades, and `Shift+←` / `Shift+→` move one second at any frame rate.
